How it works

Twenty minutes per tool. Answers you can act on the same week.

Create an outlet

One outlet is free and needs no signup. Planning two locations, or running a second branch? Pro lifts the cap and compares them side by side.

Fill in the worksheet

Real questions with real inputs — rents, covers, ingredient costs, review counts. Live calculations update as you type, so you see the effect of a number before you commit to it.

Read the verdict

Every tool scores you out of 100 and flags specific problems in plain language: "Rent is 31.8% of realistic revenue" — not "consider reviewing your costs".

Fix, re-run, export

Change an assumption and watch the score move. On Pro, save snapshots to compare versions and export a clean PDF for a partner, a landlord or a bank.

Questions

FAQ

Is the free plan actually usable, or is it a demo?

All eight tools work fully on Free, with every calculation, every score and every flagged warning. The limits are one outlet, and no PDF export, benchmarks or snapshots. Nothing is time-limited.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser, on your own device. Nothing you type into a worksheet is transmitted anywhere — which also means it will not follow you to another device and will be lost if you clear your browsing data. Export a PDF when you finish something important.

Do I need an account?

Not to use the tools. You only give an email if you want product updates or, later, to hold a Pro subscription.

Are the numbers India-specific?

Yes. Rupee amounts, aggregator commission ranges, tiffin subscription pricing, kitty party packages and Tier 2 city dynamics are built in. The underlying maths — food cost, contribution margin, break-even — works anywhere.

I am already open. Is this still useful?

That is where several tools are most useful. Menu Engineering needs real sales data to classify your Stars and Dogs, and the Dead Hours planner assumes you already know what your empty afternoons look like. Most existing restaurants find money fastest in those two plus Break-Even.

Is this financial advice?

No. The tools produce estimates from the numbers you enter, against published industry ranges. Have a lawyer read any lease and an accountant check any financial plan before you commit money.
