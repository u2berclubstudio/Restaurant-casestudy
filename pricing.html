Pricing FAQ

Will the free plan stay free?

Yes. One outlet with every tool working is the permanent free plan, not a trial. It is the version most owners planning a single restaurant will ever need.

What counts as an outlet?

One physical location or planned location. Each outlet holds its own set of eight worksheets so nothing gets mixed between them. People shortlisting two spaces usually want two outlets so they can compare.

Can I cancel?

Whenever you like. Your worksheets stay on your device either way — dropping to Free removes export, benchmarks and extra outlets, not your data.

Do you offer anything for consultants or multi-brand groups?

Yes, that is on the way — one workspace, many client outlets, white-labelled reports. Email us and we will factor your setup into how it works.
